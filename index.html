Biography :: The Sound & Presence

Classic American Songbook Elegance with an Electric Contemporary Edge

David is a Munich-based jazz vocalist whose style blends the elegance of classic crooners with the energy and immediacy of contemporary live performance. As a vocalist with a Munich big band, he has developed his sound through regular work with full horn sections, rhythm section and ensemble arrangements, performing repertoire associated with Frank Sinatra, Nat King Cole, Michael Bublé, Dean Martin, Andy Williams, Robbie Williams and the modern swing tradition.

His approach is centered on storytelling, rhythmic phrasing and a warm, resonant male vocal sound. Rather than treating the American Songbook as museum repertoire, David is interested in making standards feel direct, personal and alive for contemporary audiences.
